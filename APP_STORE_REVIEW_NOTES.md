# FlipMusic 1.0 (4) App Review Notes

## English

FlipMusic is an Apple Music library browser and player. Spotify integration and external lyrics services are not included in this build.

### Apple Music authorization and playback

1. Launch FlipMusic.
2. Tap the Settings button in the upper-right corner.
3. Tap **Connect Apple Music**.
4. Allow access to **Media & Apple Music** when the system permission dialog appears.
5. Keep the app in the foreground while the first library sync completes.
6. Return to the home screen and tap any album artwork to start playback. The bottom player can pause or resume the current song.
7. While a song is playing, send the app to the background and return to confirm playback continues at the same position.

The review device must be signed in to an Apple ID with Apple Music library content available. If the library is empty, please add songs or albums to the Apple Music library before testing. FlipMusic reads the authorized on-device Apple Music library and does not provide its own music catalog or reviewer account.

The app supports iPhone and iPad. Apple Music library data is processed on device. FlipMusic does not operate an account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or developer backend.

## 中文

FlipMusic 是 Apple Music 资料库浏览与播放工具。本版本不包含 Spotify 集成，也不使用外部歌词服务。

### Apple Music 授权与播放步骤

1. 启动 FlipMusic。
2. 点击右上角设置按钮。
3. 点击“连接 Apple Music”。
4. 在系统权限弹窗中允许访问“媒体与 Apple Music”。
5. 首次同步期间保持 App 在前台，等待资料库加载完成。
6. 返回首页，点击任意专辑封面即可播放；底部播放器可暂停和恢复当前歌曲。
7. 播放期间将 App 切到后台再返回，歌曲会保持原进度继续播放。

审核设备需要登录一个拥有 Apple Music 资料库内容的 Apple ID。若资料库为空，请先在 Apple Music 中收藏歌曲或专辑。FlipMusic 只读取用户授权的设备资料库，不提供独立音乐曲库或审核账号。

App 支持 iPhone 和 iPad。Apple Music 资料在设备内处理，App 不包含用户账号系统、分析 SDK、广告 SDK或开发者后台。
