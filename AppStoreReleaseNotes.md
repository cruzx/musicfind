# FlipMusic 1.1

## What's New

FlipMusic brings a more immersive way to listen to your Apple Music library.

- A redesigned playback page with a dynamic, colorful album-art experience.
- Artwork-derived colors that flow through the player, controls, and progress bar.
- Synchronized lyrics displayed directly in the playback page.
- Smoother card transitions and more responsive playback interactions.
- Improved playback continuity when switching songs or returning to the app.

## App Review Notes

1. Allow Apple Music access when prompted so the app can load the local media library.
2. Tap any song to open the playback page.
3. The playback page demonstrates the dynamic artwork colors, synchronized lyrics when available, and playback progress.
