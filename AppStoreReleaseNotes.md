# FlipMusic 1.2

## What's New - Simplified Chinese

全新多彩播放体验现已上线：

- 创新播放页，让专辑封面与整页色彩自然融合。
- 支持同步歌词，听歌时更沉浸。
- 新增横屏播放布局，封面与歌词各自清晰呈现。
- 新增音乐分享卡，可结合照片、歌词和当前位置创作分享图片。
- 优化播放交互、歌单切换、性能与稳定性。

## What's New - English

FlipMusic brings a more immersive way to listen to your Apple Music library.

- A redesigned playback page with a dynamic, colorful album-art experience.
- Artwork-derived colors that flow through the player, controls, and progress bar.
- Synchronized lyrics displayed directly in the playback page.
- A new landscape layout with artwork and lyrics presented side by side.
- Custom music share cards with optional photos, lyrics, and location.
- Smoother playback interactions, playlist switching, and improved stability.

## App Review Notes

1. No account or sign-in is required. Allow Apple Music access when prompted so the app can load the user's media library.
2. Tap any song to open the playback page. Rotate the device to view the dedicated landscape layout.
3. Synchronized lyrics are shown when available. To match lyrics, the app sends only the current song title, artist, album, and available duration to LRCLIB. It does not send Apple account information, the user's library, play counts, or listening history.
4. Tap the share button in the playback page to open the share composer. Camera and location access are optional and requested only after the user chooses the corresponding feature. Photo access is used only when the user saves a generated share image.
